## Quran.com itself (web) — current features

Reading: surah/juz/revelation-order lists, Arabic reading mode with word
actions, translation (book-style) mode, Tajweed Mushaf, Read in Context, pin
and compare verses, surah info from several sources, Verse of the Day/Week,
Duas, Quran Radio, Reciters page, 19 interface languages.

Study Mode: tafsir, reflections, lessons, Answers (Q&A), hadith, qira'at,
translation layers, study-content language selection, shareable deep links.

Personal: one reading bookmark + 3 named reading bookmarks, collections,
ayah notes (archive, 30-day restore, PDF export), goals and streaks, reading
history calendar, in-app notifications, preferences sync.

Programs: Learning Plans (40+, no login), Quran in a Year (7 stations,
Friday email, Ask a Question), Ramadan plans, Topics.

Media: Quran Media Maker (verse videos/images, 15+ video backgrounds).

Community: Reflect button, like/follow, Re-Reflect, QuranReflect, Quran Space.

Developer: Content/User/QuranReflect APIs, OAuth2, Quran MCP, open source.
